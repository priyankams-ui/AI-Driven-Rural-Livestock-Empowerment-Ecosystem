Dairy Livestock in Karnataka

Understanding cow and buffalo farming, milk yields, and sustainable dairy practices.

COWS (ಹಸು)

Milk Production & Characteristics

Cows form the backbone of Karnataka's dairy industry. High-yielding crossbreeds like Holstein Friesian (HF) and Jersey produce between 15 to 28+ litres per day, while indigenous breeds offer disease resistance and rich A2 milk.

Breeding & Management

Healthy breeding management requires regular lactation cycle tracking, balanced nutritional feed (green fodder, dry roughage, and mineral mixture), and routine veterinary vaccinations.

  • High daily milk yield and consistent lactation curve
  • Popular breeds: Holstein Friesian, Jersey, Red Dane, Brown Swiss
  • Adaptable to diverse Karnataka agro-climatic zones

BUFFALOES (ಎಮ್ಮೆ)

Milk Production & Characteristics

Buffalo milk is renowned for its high butterfat content (6.5% to 8.5%), making it highly valuable for premium milk products, curd, ghee, and dairy cooperatives across Karnataka.

Breeding & Management

Buffaloes exhibit superior feed conversion efficiency for roughage and coarse fodder. Notable breeds like Murrah, Surti, and Jaffarabadi thrive in irrigated river basin regions and require wallowing facilities or cool shelter during peak summer.

  • High fat percentage (6.5% - 8.5%) yielding higher milk price per litre
  • Prominent breeds: Murrah, Surti, Jaffarabadi, Mehsana, Pandharpuri
  • Excellent longevity and disease resistance
